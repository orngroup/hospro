HOSPRO ACCESS: EMAIL VERIFICATION SET-UP (one-off, about 5 minutes)
==================================================================

What changed
- New devices no longer email Ajay for approval. The person fills in their details
  (IP detected automatically), accepts the terms of use, and Firebase emails them a
  secure one-time link. Clicking it verifies their email, records the access and
  takes them to the normal staff login. No approval needed.
- Every verification is saved to Firestore (collection: access_log) and shown in
  HOSPRO under Settings > Device access log.
- Devices approved under the old flow stay approved.
- Staff still sign in with their own login after verifying, as before.

1. FIREBASE CONSOLE (project brandonhall-7bdef)
   a) Authentication > Sign-in method > Email/Password > switch on
      "Email link (passwordless sign-in)" > Save.
   b) Authentication > Settings > Authorised domains > make sure
      brandon.hospro.co.uk is listed (add it if not).
   c) Optional: Authentication > Templates > set the sender name to "HOSPRO".
   d) Firestore Database > Rules > replace everything with the contents of
      firestore.rules from this folder > Publish.
      The rules give full access only to the four staff logins listed in them.
      When you add a staff member, add their login email to that list too.

2. GITHUB (repo orngroup/brandonme)
   Upload app.js, index.html, sw.js and firestore.rules, replacing the old ones.

3. TEST
   Open brandon.hospro.co.uk in a private window, fill in the form with your
   own email, accept the terms and press "Email my verification link".
   Open the email on the same device and tap the link. You should land on the
   staff login, and see yourself in Settings > Device access log once signed in.

FOR THE NEXT HOTEL
   At the top of app.js, change TENANT (hotel name, place, terms version).
   The access screen, terms and access log all update from that one setting.
   Optionally set TENANT.allowedDomains, e.g. ["newhotel.co.uk"], so only
   staff email addresses can verify.
   Update the staff login emails in firestore.rules for that hotel's project.
