🔥Fire & Emergency — Brandon Hall
Call 999 on every activation — never assume a monitoring service has called. Simultaneous evacuation: everyone leaves when the alarm sounds. Never use the lift.
📍 Assembly point
Out of the front door, turn right, across the car park.
🗣️ What to say on 999
"Fire at Brandon Hall Hotel and Spa, Main Street, Brandon, Coventry CV8 3FW. The panel shows [zone / location]."

🛎️ Reception — when the alarm sounds

  1. Treat every activation as genuine. Stop normal duties; note the zone on the panel if safe — never delay evacuation to investigate. Do not silence, reset or isolate unless authorised.
  2. Tell the duty manager and marshals using the hotel's emergency communication arrangements.
  3. Call 999 — Fire and Rescue, every time. Give hotel name, address, zone, any smoke or fire, people at risk and a callback number.
  4. Warn and direct guests calmly to the safe exits and assembly point — never the lifts.
  5. Take the records — only if safe: occupancy report, staff sign-in, contractor and visitor records. Never delay leaving to fetch paperwork.
  6. Leave by a safe route. Do not stay at reception for calls or go to check rooms. Never enter smoke.
  7. At the assembly point give the duty manager the records, panel details and anyone needing help. Report anyone missing and their last location — nobody goes back in.

🔥 If you discover a fire

  1. Raise the alarm — break the nearest red call point.
  2. Get out, close doors behind you to hold the fire back.
  3. Only tackle it if safe — small fire, you are trained, and your way out is behind you; otherwise leave it.
  4. Report to the duty manager at the fire alarm panel or the assembly point.

🌙 At night (2 staff)

  1. Night staff 1: panel → identify zone → call 999 → guest list to the assembly point → meet the fire service.
  2. Night staff 2: sweep bedroom wings from the affected zone outwards, knocking on doors. Help guests with a PEEP.
  3. Working alone: call 999 first, then the panel, then wake the affected wing. Tell the fire service what has not been checked.

🧖 Spa, pool & leisure (Granite Leisure)

  1. Granite Leisure staff evacuate their areas under their own procedure.
  2. Clear the pool hall, sauna, steam room and changing rooms first — robes and towels for guests outside.
  3. They report to the hotel duty manager at the assembly point with the spa sign-in list. Contact: Aiden — 07301 067739.

🔁 After every activation

  1. Do not reset until the cause is found — duty manager checks the zone first.
  2. Record it: HosFIRE → Record a check → Fire alarm event.
  3. Repeat false alarm or fault? Report to Leader Systems the same day: 01788 542 220.
Source: Fire Action Procedure (BHH-ST-02) & Fire Safety Manual v1.1.
