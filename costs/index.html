Update the data

In Xero run Account Transactions for Bar Costs (Drinks), Restaurant Costs and Room Costs (Linen and Materials), export each to Excel, then load the files here.

The page refreshes immediately on your screen. To make the update permanent for everyone, replace costs/data/costs-data.js in the GitHub repository with the downloaded file.

Each upload replaces all lines for the accounts it contains, so always export the full period. Category rules live in costs-engine.js. The market prices and written findings on the Savings tab are a dated review and do not change with an upload.
