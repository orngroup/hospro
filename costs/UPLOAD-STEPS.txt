HosBUSINESS v14 — upload steps

Two things to do: deploy the feed update, then upload the 4 website files.

============================================================
JOB 1 — Deploy the feed update (3 minutes, Cloud Shell)
============================================================

1. Open shell.cloud.google.com.
2. Click the three dots (top right of the black area) → Upload.
3. Choose guestline-feed-v3.zip.
4. Paste this into Cloud Shell and press Enter:

   cd ~ && unzip -o guestline-feed-v3.zip && cd guestline-feed && npm --prefix functions install && firebase deploy --only functions:guestline --project brandonhall-7bdef --force

5. Wait 2-3 minutes for "Deploy complete!"

This extends the feed with recentItems so the website can show what sold.

============================================================
JOB 2 — Upload the 4 website files (2 minutes, GitHub)
============================================================

1. Open github.com/orngroup/hospro → "costs" folder.
2. Click "Add file" → "Upload files".
3. Drag all FOUR files from THIS folder into the box:
     home.js
     index.html
     guestline-feed.js
     hosbusiness-v13.js
4. Commit message: Add items drilldown + Trading date filters
5. Click the green "Commit changes" button.
6. Wait 60 seconds, then Cmd+Shift+R on brandon.hospro.co.uk/costs.

WHAT'S NEW IN v14
============================================================

NEW: Items drilldown on the POS page
- A "What sold on [date]" block at the bottom of POS, grouped by outlet (Restaurant food, Restaurant drinks, Breakfast, Bar food, Bar drinks, Room service, Functions, Conference).
- Shows top 10 items per group with quantity, net value and average price.
- Uses the items for the last closed day (the feed returns ~450 item lines on an average day).
- "Posted by hand" means a hand-typed posting - we keep the group but not the description, so no guest names leak.

NEW: Date range on the Trading tab
- A new summary block at the top: Rooms sold, ADR, RevPAR, TRevPAR, Total revenue, Out of service.
- Driven by the same date range chips as the F&B POS (Today, Yesterday, Last 7/14/21/28 days, Month to date, Last month, Last 90 days, Year to date, Last 12 months, Custom).
- Compare dropdown: Previous period or Same period last year.
- The month selector below is kept for drill-down.

NEW: Compare on Trading
- When Compare is set to "Previous period" or "Same period last year", every tile shows the +/- percentage vs the comparison range.
- Also works on the F&B POS tiles.
