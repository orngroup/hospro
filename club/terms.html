The Clarendon Club

Membership terms and privacy notice · last updated 25 November 2026

In short: membership is free for over-18s. You get a £5 welcome voucher to use within 30 days, and 5% of what you pay for food and drink at The Clarendon comes back as credit, ready from the next day and valid for 60 days. Credit can't be combined with other offers or discounts, including our 15% food offer, and has no cash value.

1. Joining

  1. The Clarendon Club is run by Brandon Hall Hotel & Spa (“we”, “us”). Membership is free.
  2. You must be 18 or over and give your own name, email address and UK mobile number. One membership per person.
  3. Your membership card is the code on your own phone. It is personal to you and can't be transferred, shared or sold.

2. Welcome voucher

  1. New members receive a £5 welcome voucher. It can be used from the moment you join and must be used within 30 days.
  2. Only one welcome voucher is given per person, email address and mobile number, including if you leave and rejoin.
  3. It can be used in full or in part on one or more bills while it is valid.

3. Earning credit

  1. You earn credit worth 5% of the amount you pay for food and drink in The Clarendon restaurant and bar at Brandon Hall, after any club credit or other discount.
  2. Credit is not earned on service charge or gratuities, gift voucher purchases, accommodation, spa services, weddings, private events, deposits or packages.
  3. Your code must be scanned by a member of staff when you pay. We can't add credit afterwards, except at a manager's discretion.
  4. Credit you earn can be spent from the day after your visit.

4. Using credit

  1. Show your code before you pay and tell your server how much credit you would like to use. You can use any amount up to your available balance.
  2. When you use credit or your welcome voucher, no other offer or discount can be applied to the same bill, including our 15% food offer.
  3. Credit can't be used towards service charge, gratuities or gift vouchers. It has no cash value and no change is given.
  4. We always use the credit that expires soonest first.

5. Expiry

  1. Each amount of credit expires 60 days after the visit on which it was earned. The welcome voucher expires 30 days after you join.
  2. Expired credit is removed from your card and can't be restored.
  3. Your card shows when your next credit is due to expire.

6. Members' offers

From time to time we show offers on your card. Each offer has its own conditions, shown with the offer. Offers can't be combined with each other unless the offer says so.

7. Changes, misuse and ending the scheme

  1. We may change these terms, the rate of credit or the expiry periods. We'll give at least 30 days' notice of a change that reduces your benefits, on your card or by email. Credit already earned keeps the terms it was earned under.
  2. We may suspend or close a membership, and remove its credit, if we reasonably believe it has been misused, for example by sharing a code or a fraudulent transaction.
  3. If we end The Clarendon Club we'll give at least 30 days' notice so you can use your credit.
  4. You can close your membership at any time from Account in your card. Any credit and vouchers are then lost.

8. Your privacy

What we collect: the details you give when you join (title, name, email, mobile, and if you choose, birthday day and month, postcode, how you know us, how often you dine out, occasions and dietary preference), and a record of your visits: date, bill total, credit earned and used, and the staff member who served you. We don't store allergy information on your card; please tell your server each time you dine.

Why: to run your membership and card (performing our agreement with you); to understand how members use The Clarendon so we can improve it (our legitimate interests); and, only if you tick the box, to email you members' offers and news (your consent). Offer alerts on your phone are only sent if you switch them on.

Who sees it: Brandon Hall management and restaurant staff who need it. It is stored on secure cloud services provided by Google, which act on our instructions. We don't sell your details.

How long: while you are a member. If you close your membership we delete your personal details, keeping only anonymised transaction records for our accounts. If a membership is unused for 24 months we may close it.

Your rights: you can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or object to how we use them. Change email preferences in Account, or ask at reception. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

9. General

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales. Nothing in them affects your statutory rights.
