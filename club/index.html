My account

When can I use my credit?

Credit you earn is ready from the day after your visit and lasts 60 days. Your welcome voucher can be used straight away and lasts 30 days. We always use whatever expires first.

Can I use it with other offers?

No. When you use credit or a voucher, other discounts, including our 15% food offer, can't be added to the same bill.

What does it cover?

Food and drink in The Clarendon restaurant and bar at Brandon Hall. Not service charge, gift vouchers, accommodation or spa treatments. Credit has no cash value.
